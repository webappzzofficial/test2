ABOUT NDT

BUILT FOR
DEMANDING WORK.

NDT — Nabeel Dar Transport LLC is a UAE-based provider of heavy equipment rental, crane rental, heavy lifting and transportation solutions, serving projects across Dubai and the wider United Arab Emirates.

Specializing in mobile crane rental and heavy lifting, NDT supports construction, infrastructure, oil & gas, industrial, marine and heavy-engineering operations with dependable equipment and project-focused support.

Our capabilities extend beyond equipment rental. We support customers with lifting, rigging, heavy transportation, machinery positioning, equipment mobilisation and technical project requirements.

From short-term equipment hire to long-term project support, our approach is centered on operational reliability, safety, responsive service and efficient project execution.
